The six chords

Numbers = fret per string, left to right; 0 = open, x = don't play. Baritone uke (DGBE): use the guitar shape on the top four strings.

  1. G

  2. Em

  3. C

  4. D

  5. Am

  6. F

    guitar: easy Fmaj7

Strums

  • easy (songs 1, 2) 1 2 3 4 → D D D D
  • island (songs 2, 4, 5) 1 & 2 & 3 & 4 & → D · D U · U D U
  • 6/8 oar (song 3) 1 2 3 4 5 6 → D · U D · U
