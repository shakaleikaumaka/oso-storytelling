How to join

Just come

Open jams and lessons never need an application. Bring anything with strings, a drum, a voice, or nothing at all.

Six chords

G · Em · C · D · Am · F on ukulele or guitar carry every song in Lesson 1. Shapes, strums and the order we learn them are on each sheet.

Consent first

One voice each; recordings stay private until everyone says yes. Photos and names go public only with every person's yes. Takedown first.

Curated slots & the rider

Want a set at a concert edition, or to host a circle at your own gathering? The open form: a rotating circle of stewards answers everyone within 14 days.
