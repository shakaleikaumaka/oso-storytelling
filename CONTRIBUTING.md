# 🎻 Contributing to the Open Source Orchestra site

**Yes — PRs are open to every human on Earth.** 🌍

This repo isn't a mirror. **This repo IS the website.** When a PR merges to `main`, opensourceorchestra.org updates automatically within ~1 minute (GitHub Pages). What you merge is what the world sees.

## How to play
1. **Fork** this repo 🍴 (forking isn't stealing — it's how open music travels)
2. Make your change — fix a typo, translate a definition, tune the design, improve accessibility, add a verse
3. Open a **Pull Request** — the stewards review right here on GitHub and merge with aloha
4. Watch it go live at [opensourceorchestra.org](https://opensourceorchestra.org) 🎶

## The house rules (the only rules)
- **The manifesto's heart stays verbatim** — philosophy changes come through the committee, not PRs. Typos, code, design, translations: always welcome.
- **No names on the page** — the site speaks as a community. Credit lives in commit history.
- **Code is GPL-3.0-or-later** (copyleft: share-alike for code) — by contributing code, you agree it is licensed that way. **Words, art and music stay CC0** (or their makers' own terms) — no rights reserved unless the maker says otherwise. See `LICENSE`, `LICENSE-CONTENT` and `NOTICE`.
- **Heart first, always.** 🌺

*You don't join the Open Source Orchestra. You notice you were always in it.*
