La historia · the story

Una silla para toda una comunidad 🪑

013 — the builder's number, and a whole community in one chair

This node is a first: every chair before it belongs to one person. Node 013 belongs to a collective — because sometimes the voice that joins the orchestra is a hundred and fifty voices that already learned to sing as one. The digital root of 13 is 4: the builder's number, four corners, foundations — right for the node that turns "a chair for an artist" into "a chair for a movement." (And the orchestra smiles at the echo: the founding songbook of Node 001 held exactly 13 songs.)

Quiénes son · who they are

In their own words: "MusicaW3 es una comunidad internacional de artistas independientes y un club social dedicado al desarrollo de carreras de artistas independientes de distintas partes del mundo" — an international community of independent artists, focused on creating opportunities and strong bonds between everyone who believes collective construction is the main engine (from musicaw3.com). Born from Argentina; founded by 0xGonzalo, NENX and Sol Siete (their site's FOUNDERS section).

What they do: monthly collective drops pairing established web3 artists with new talents; WOW! Festival de Arte y Tecnología — live music, interactive installations, talks, and "cultura al servicio de la comunidad" — culture in service of community; and their own tools for distributing digital art. In partnership with Fundación Blockchain, Oasis and Arbitrum.

La escuela de la soberanía 🎓

100% gratuito · principiantes → avanzados · toward the sovereignty of onchain creators

Their education program is free for everyone and titled like a manifesto: "Hacia la Soberanía de los Creadores Onchain." That is the same song the orchestra's node spec sings — the artist owns the work, the data, the door — in Spanish, taught to hundreds, for free.

La silla está reservada 🇦🇷 — for all of you

MusicaW3 — esta silla es de ustedes. This chair holds 150 artists — and each of you is a future node of your own. The Music Space at Devcon 8, Mumbai, Nov 3–6 2026 (DIP #8576) is the biggest circle the orchestra has drawn, and a community that already fills festivals with "cultura al servicio de la comunidad" belongs inside it. Come as a delegation, come as a drop, come as one song carried across the ocean — nothing is asked, everything is offered. Two of the roads between us are already walked: Argentina gave the orchestra its Devconnect Music Space, and gave the world Dúo Dø (Nodes 010 & 011).
