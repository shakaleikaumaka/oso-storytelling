Genre-free and in motion

Born in Buenos Aires, 2015 — in their words, more than a band: "un espacio de experimentación constante entre dos compañeros de vida" — a constant experiment between two companions in life (their words, from duodomusica.com). Argentine roots in dialogue with Japan: Todo Cambia with koto master Hiroe Morikawa, the lo-fi samurai single Torii, and now a life lived on the road between the two worlds. Releases include Yama (ft. Dig.Dug, 2026), Piano Works 1 (EP, 2025) and Nostalgia (2025).

Japan Tour 2026: September 19 → December 15 — 15 confirmed shows across Tokyo, Kobuchizawa, Kesennuma, Kawamata (Cosquín Japón), Osaka and Kyoto, plus a masterclass at the Osaka College of Music.

The Ethereum thread 🌺

Devconnect Buenos Aires → SongCamp Latam → Kismet Casa, Rome → the Infinite Garden

In 2025 Dúo Dø was invited to lead the SongCamp Challenge Latam — three artists, one week, one question: what is Latin American identity? The EP was presented on The Creator Economy stage during Devconnect Argentina (their own site's telling — the same Devconnect Buenos Aires where the orchestra's Music Space lived at La Rural, Nov 2025).

In 2026 they were artists-in-residence at Kismet Casa during FarCon Rome, and the residency became a film: Mi Amor Por Ti (ft. Matt Lee) — "some songs are written; ours was lived within a community." In its final credits, among the Executive Producers who made it real, one name the orchestra knows well: Shaka 🌺 (verified in the film's published credits). Their work has also been shown with the Infinite Garden Artist Cohort — the Ethereum Foundation's own garden of artists.

Kindred movement 🇦🇷🌐

The Argentina Dúo Dø comes from is also home to MUSICA W3 — an international community of 150+ independent web3 artists and 2k+ collectors, born from Argentina, whose free education program is literally titled "Hacia la Soberanía de los Creadores Onchain" — toward the sovereignty of onchain creators. That is the orchestra's node gospel, sung in Spanish. Same song, kindred circle: Node 013 · MUSICA W3 — the orchestra's first collective chair.
