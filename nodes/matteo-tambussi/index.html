The story

The brother who asked first 🎻

The first DIP in Devcon history

The Open Source Orchestra tradition was born at ETH Prague 2022 — and on June 17, 2022, Matteo carried it to paper: he submitted the first music DIP ever proposed for a Devcon — "Music Sessions | Open Mic Stage" for Devcon 6 Bogotá. His argument was simple and right: jamming live amongst devs, product managers and community leaders creates bonds no whitepaper can — and "many many devs are actually f**king good musicians." The Music Space exists because he asked for it first.

The lineage he seeded now runs to DIP #8576 — the Music Space at Devcon 8, Mumbai, Nov 3–6 2026.

"None of this would be possible without you, brother." — Shaka 🌺

Twenty-five years of song

Musician and producer in the independent scene since 2000 — first ten years with the bands Baroque and Eskinzo, then solo from Berlin (2013 →) and home again in Turin. Eleven-plus albums; opening slots for Nick Cave & the Bad Seeds, Primal Scream, Boy George, Blonde Redhead, Africa Unite, Ministri; radio play on BBC 6 Music, J-Wave Tokyo, Flux FM and Fritz. His duo record Zilina (Calista Records, 2017) was made with Marek K — the same Marek riding shotgun on Pharmacist Wife below. (sources: Athens Music Week · Blockchain Forum Italia)

A political linguist by study and an ethereum ant by nature: in the Ethereum world since Devcon4 Prague and Dappcon 2018, co-organizer of Italy's first Ethereum hackathon ETHTurin (2020), co-founder of SpaghettETH — and a speaker on music × web3 from Athens Music Week to Most Wanted: Music Berlin to the World Art Forum. Matteo has been weaving music into Ethereum since before it had a stage.
