Node 001

Matteo Tambussi 🎻🇮🇹

The brother who asked first

Co-founder of the Open Source Orchestra tradition — born at ETH Prague 2022, alive in the circles of Devcon ever since.

On June 17, 2022, Matteo submitted the first music DIP in Devcon history — “Music Sessions | Open Mic Stage” for Devcon Bogotá — arguing that jamming live amongst devs creates bonds no whitepaper can. He was right. The Music Space exists because he asked for it first.

Musician, linguistics passionate, web3 OG from Turin, Italy — an ethereum ant at work on many industry intersections and co-founder of SpaghettETH, Matteo has been weaving music into Ethereum since before it had a stage.

“None of this would be possible without you, brother.” — Shaka 🌺

Node 004

Manu Alzuru 🎧🥑

El Cuatro — four on the floor, or so much more

DJ, producer, and community architect — the founder and conspirator of Blockravers, the pop-up rave family born at ETHBarcelona out of a hundred “where do we go tonight?” messages, now carrying dance floors to crypto gatherings, pop-up cities, and festivals around the world — Grimes, Chloe Caillet, Android Jones, Desert Dwellers, and countless unnamed dancers.

An engineer turned lovepunk — the force behind DoinGud, ETHBarcelona, MetaCartel, Api3 — Manu has spent years proving that decentralization is something you can feel: in music, in movement, in a room full of strangers becoming one crowd.

From the very beginning, he and Shaka shared a heart for doing good in this world with the technology. Now the orchestra opens its fourth chair so the world can also hear and sing.

“TQM Manu!! El cuatro was always yours.” — Shaka 🌺

The invitation 💌

Hermano — this seat is yours. The Open Source Orchestra is drawing its biggest circle yet: the Music Space at Devcon 8, Mumbai, Nov 3–6 2026 (DIP #8576). We would be honored to have Node 004 in the circle — your sound, your Blockravers spirit, your four-on-the-floor heart.

The orchestra is open source in every sense: no gate, no audition, no title. Bring a track, bring a set, bring your people — or simply bring yourself. Every voice is a node, and this one has been humming since before we had a stage.

The open call is live if you want to put your name in the circle — or just say the word and we'll hold your chair. 🎧
