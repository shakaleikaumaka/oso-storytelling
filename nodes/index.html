Node 001

Matteo Tambussi 🎻🇮🇹

The brother who asked first

Co-founder of the Open Source Orchestra tradition — born at ETH Prague 2022, alive in the circles of Devcon ever since.

On June 17, 2022, Matteo submitted the first music DIP in Devcon history — “Music Sessions | Open Mic Stage” for Devcon Bogotá — arguing that jamming live amongst devs creates bonds no whitepaper can. He was right. The Music Space exists because he asked for it first.

Musician, linguistics passionate, web3 OG from Turin, Italy — an ethereum ant at work on many industry intersections and co-founder of SpaghettETH, Matteo has been weaving music into Ethereum since before it had a stage.

“None of this would be possible without you, brother.” — Shaka 🌺

Node 002

Justin Holmes 🪕⛓️

Blockchains are bluegrass

Leader of Justin Holmes and the Immutable String Band — in their own words, “songs of a peaceful internet age, video game traditionals, and dirty old bluegrass,” played live at tech events around the world. He named the movement cryptograss: bluegrass and blockchains as one tradition of preserving, remixing and passing music hand to hand outside the “intellectual property” system.

His album Vowel Sounds (2023, Nashville — with Cory Walker and Harry Clark of East Nash Grass) was revealed on-chain through Threshold’s Revealer, “more in keeping with the traditional public domain model of Appalachian music.” Before the band: years building privacy tech at NuCypher, banjo never far from the keyboard. His releases are CC BY-SA — kin of the orchestra’s CC0 heart.

“Blockchains are bluegrass.” — Node 002 🪕

Manu Alzuru 🎧🥑

El Cuatro — four on the floor, or so much more

DJ, producer, and community architect — the founder and conspirator of Blockravers, the pop-up rave family born at ETHBarcelona out of a hundred “where do we go tonight?” messages, now carrying dance floors to crypto gatherings, pop-up cities, and festivals around the world — Grimes, Chloe Caillet, Android Jones, Desert Dwellers, and countless unnamed dancers.

Node 014

Inch Chua 🍃⭕

THE FREEHAND CIRCLE — the first Singapore chair

At a conformist all-girls' school, the class drew circles with compasses. One kid just whipped hers off freehand — "That's not a real circle!" the others cried, and she answered: "You know, not everything has to be perfect." That kid became Inch Chua (iNCH): folktronica pioneer, the first Singaporean solo artist ever invited to SXSW (2010), who put The Bedroom EP on Bandcamp free / pay-what-you-want in 2009 — open source before web3 had a name.

The second circle widens to Southeast Asia: Node 014 is the first Singapore chair, and the Devcon SEA season — Bangkok's Music & Art Space — is where the orchestra's road crossed hers. As Shaka tells it, the chair was never in doubt: "it was inch." (And quietly: 1 + 4 = 5 — the power fifth, humming an octave up.)

Not everything has to be perfect. It just has to be sung. ⭕
