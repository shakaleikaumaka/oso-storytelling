Featured composition

The circle that faces inward 🔄

stage → circle · audience → voices · performance → consensus

Tomo's vision, as Shaka carries it: the Devcon music circle should face inward — no stage and crowd, just people looking at each other, singing. And there is a reason the vision points at India: in the orchestra's whole travelled life, India is the only country where a circle spontaneously became hundreds of people singing together — and it happened twice (as Shaka tells it). Devcon 8 is in Mumbai. The country that already knows how to do this is hosting the orchestra's biggest circle yet.

That is a composition the way trustgraphs is a composition: not notes on a page, but a shape that makes music happen. The season of the Every Voice a Node conversations is planned to close on exactly this — the inward-facing circle, live at Devcon 8, audience singing, recorded, archived, forked — the way the haiku promised.
