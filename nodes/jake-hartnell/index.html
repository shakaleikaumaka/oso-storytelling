The story

The power 5th ⚡

Root + fifth = power chord

A power chord is the simplest strong thing in music: the root and its fifth, nothing else — and suddenly the melody has weight. The orchestra had its melody: songs given away, circles at Devcon, every voice a node. Jake is the fifth. His lane is the trust layer — the mathematics of who vouches for whom, proven so cleanly that nobody has to take anyone's word for the word-taking itself.

The orchestra met Jake on a Colorado mountain night in August 2026 — and started building with him that same night, in the open, where all real jams happen.

"A power chord is root + fifth. Jake is the fifth." — Shaka 🌺

Featured composition

trustgraphs — a bar in four beats 🕸️

VOUCH → SCORE → PROVE → USE · one bar, repeated until the community is a chord

trustgraphs turns a community's web of vouches into reputation scores anyone can verify and fake accounts can't inflate — no trusted operator, just a zero-knowledge proof. Members vouch, trust-aware PageRank scores, an SP1 proof makes the math unfalsifiable, and governance weight or reward splits consume the proven root. Sybil bot-armies vouching for themselves stay islands no trusted edge ever reaches.
