The room you let us build

वह कमरा, जो आपने हमें बनाने दिया · तुम्ही आम्हाला उभी करू दिलेली खोली

For four days at Devcon 8 in Mumbai, the Devcon team gave us a room: cane stools, drums on rugs and a mandala on the wall, open to anyone who wanted to play. We made them a little book of their own photographers' pictures of that room, 28 pages captioned in English, हिन्दी and मराठी. We sent it to them first. Now it is here for everyone who sat in the circle. 🦋
