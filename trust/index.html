How it plays

Vouch → Score → Prove → Use

1 · Vouch 🎶

After a jam, musicians vouch on-chain: "I played with this human — they showed up, they listened." Each vouch is a public attestation (EAS) — an edge in the graph above.

2 · Score 🌊

Trust-Aware PageRank lets trust flow outward from the founding seeds. The more real, trusted paths reach you, the brighter you glow. This page runs the real algorithm live.

3 · Prove ⚡

No operator computes the scores. Anyone can — and a zero-knowledge proof (SP1) convinces the chain the computation used every vouch, exactly. Correct scores, or no proof at all.

4 · Use 🎻

The proven scores suggest superfan chairs in the songbook, weight community decisions, and could someday rotate the orchestra's keys — all without a gatekeeper.

🪑 Together: trust proposes, the artist disposes

Trust scores suggest who has earned a superfan chair; the artist still appoints every one. Community wisdom flows in, artist sovereignty stays sacred — invariant untouched. And because seeds anchor the graph, an open orchestra can stay radically open: a thousand bots vouching for each other are just a dark island no real edge reaches.
