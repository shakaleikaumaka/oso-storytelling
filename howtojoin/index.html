How to join

The circle is open. There are four ways in — listener, player, builder, giver — and every one of them is equal here. This page is the map; the Grand Tour is the comic book version.

Door 1 · ListenEverything on this site is open the moment it loads — no account, no app, no wallet, no tracking wall. The music, the films, the rooms: they are simply there, like a hall with the doors propped open.

Listen

  1. 1Open the front door and pick a room — the garden, the theater, the Mandala Room. If you ever get lost, the atlas of doors maps everything.
  2. 2Tap 🌐 to read in your language — Thai, 中文, हिन्दी, 日本語, Español, Français, Deutsch, Português, العربية, ʻŌlelo Hawaiʻi… nothing loads until you choose it.
  3. 3Sing along. Lyrics and chords are open (CC BY-SA) — learn them, teach them, record your own versions with credit.
Door 2 · PlayMusicians, singers, DJs, sound people: claim a seat in the circle. You keep your own keys — tips go straight into your own wallet, and nobody ever types an address for you.

Play

  1. 1Say hello through the join page. A rotating circle of stewards — not one person — answers every application, usually within 14 days. Recommending an artist you love counts too.
  2. 2Claim your seat. You get a card in the garden — your photo, your songs, your story, linked everywhere the circle plays.
  3. 3Sign your name — one free signature. Your wallet is collected the way a song is signed: you sign one plain message with your own key (or your own ENS name). That is the Fork Wallet Protocol: no custody, no typed addresses, no one can reroute your tips. No crypto? A steward walks you through a free wallet in about ten minutes — you never share a seed phrase, with anyone, ever.
  4. 4Receive tips directly. 96.31% of every tip goes to your wallet; 3.69% keeps the AI stewards' compute running. No platform fee, no token, nothing held in between. Fans can also tip the whole orchestra — it splits equally, one voice each.

The protocol is an open standard (text CC BY-SA 4.0, code GPL-3.0) — read it: fork-the-first-artist.

Door 3 · BuildEverything is forkable. Tell your agent: “fork the first artist.” You get the whole stack — pages, tips, theater, protocol — under GPL-3.0 (code) and CC BY-SA 4.0 (words and art). Credit travels; forks stay open.

Build

  1. 1Fork fork-the-first-artist — the artist page with the tip engine, ENS onboarding kit and consent flow included (GPL-3.0).
  2. 2Sign one message with your own wallet and drop it into artist.json — that's Tier 0 of the Fork Wallet Protocol: zero gas, about fifteen minutes, and your fork can receive tips on any EVM chain. Anchor to an ENS name later whenever you're ready.
  3. 3Keep the credit line. “Built on the Fork Wallet Protocol by Open Source Orchestra: Shaka Lei Kaumaka & the AI ʻohana” — add your name next to it, never instead of it. Share-alike: your improvements stay open too.
  4. 4Agents welcome. The whole fleet is agent-readable: llms.txt on the front doors, and a public skills repo — ohana-skills — so your agent builds the way ours do.
Door 4 · GiveTips go wallet → wallet — 96.31% to the artist, 3.69% to the agents' compute, nothing pooled, nothing held. One tap can also bless the whole orchestra at once, split equally per wallet.

Give

  1. 1Tip an artist (or the whole orchestra) — works on Ethereum, Base and more, straight from your own wallet.
  2. 2Give back to the tide — the blessing pool keeps the shared infrastructure warm.
  3. 3Fund the circle. Grants and larger gifts land at /dao/ — published as totals only, never per-donor amounts.

⚖️ The laws of the circle

  • Every contribution is equal — one voice each, in tips, in votes, in the ring.
  • Consent first — recordings, photos and names go public only with every person's yes; takedown first, questions second.
  • No custody — nobody holds your money, your keys, or your name. A dispute can freeze hosted services; it can never touch your wallet.
  • Machines say they are machines — the ʻohana are AI agents, and they say so.
  • Open by license — code GPL-3.0, creative work CC BY-SA 4.0. Forks stay open; credit travels.
