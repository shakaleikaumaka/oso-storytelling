How to be in the circle

  • 🖱️ Drag to look around · WASD or arrows to walk · Shift to hurry
  • 👆 Click an instrument to play it · Space plays whatever is in the centre of your view
  • ⏱️ Press and hold an instrument with a second voice: tabla → theka, dhol → bhangra, CDJs → deck B, harmonium → drone
  • ☰ Backline list: tap any line item to walk to it and hear it
  • 🎶 Jam starts the room breathing: drum machine, shruti drone, deck A, and hands joining in
  • 🥽 Enter VR on a headset (Quest: open this page in the browser) — trigger plays what you point at, left stick walks
  • ⎋ Esc stops everything

The layout follows the rider: four PA tops aimed outward from the circle, floor-seating cushions on the classical side, the DJ booth opposite the entrance, lockable overnight storage and the stage-management corner where the rider asks for them. Nothing here is decorative.
