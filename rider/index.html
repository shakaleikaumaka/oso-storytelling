Space & stage — Sanctuary Tech

The stage is circular and non-directional. There is no "front." Performers face one another; the audience wraps around the music. Warmth, belonging, refuge — a home with clear boundaries and an open invitation.

§ 1.5

The circle, drawn

How to read the circle

  • ◎ The performance circle — Ø 6 m proposed, defined by rugs and light, not barriers. Walk in from any side.
  • 🥁 Kit & sub at one pole — the loudest source sits opposite the floor-seated classical players. Acoustic kit TBD
  • 🪕 Floor-seating quadrant (teal) — tabla, harmonium, bansuri on rugs & cushions, condenser-mic'd.
  • ▣ 4× powered tops at the quadrants, firing outward — even 360° coverage at low level. Proposed
  • ◢ 4 wedges + side-fill: performers spread wide in a circle need their own mixes.
  • ⏨ FOH at the perimeter with sightline into the ring, on hardline internet for the stream.
  • ⚡ Power drops at every station — nobody runs a cable across the circle. Ever.

§ 7.5

The instruments of the circle

Merged from OSO v0 The Instruments of the Circle — who can play what, how quickly anyone can join, and why the circle has a seat for every level of every hand. A permissionless orchestra doesn't mean everyone plays everything — it means everyone holds a real seat at their own level. The master and the first-timer keep the same taal.

🪔 Tuned Aug 4 with Aakash: bare minimum = harmonium · tabla · bansuri; tanpura, santoor, violin & sarod released with love (high-maintenance / rare players); sitar = bring-your-own watch until the musician walkthroughs.

Tier One · Everyone plays

First sound in minutes

The basket at the door. Hand one to anyone, and within one minute they are keeping time — the most permissionless instruments in the world.

Kartalकरताल
RhythmJoin: minutes

Hand cymbals of the bhajan tradition — if you can clap, you can play kartal. First sound: strike on beat one of the taal, and simply stay there. You are now the circle's pulse.

Manjiraमंजीरा
RhythmJoin: minutes

Small cymbals with a shimmering ring — the sparkle on kirtan and bhajan for centuries. First sound: small strikes, off the beat. Let the ring do the work.

Shruti Boxश्रुति बॉक्स
DroneJoin: minutes

Cheap, unbreakable, the most decentralized instrument there is — anyone can hold the ground. First sound: steady bellows, one note. You've become the home every melody returns to.

Dholढोल
RhythmJoin: an afternoon

Pure celebration — the barrel drum that makes it impossible to stand still. First sound: the basic dha-ge-na groove comes in an afternoon; mastery is lifelong. Joining is immediate.

Harmoniumहारमोनियम
Melody & keysJoin: minutes

The great unifier — holds the key, anchors the singers, and every classical singer in India grew up beside one. First sound: if you've ever touched a keyboard, you're holding the sa-pa drone for the whole circle in minutes.

Tier Two · An afternoon to join

Quick to join · deep to master

The welcoming middle — folk instruments with forgiving hands. Grooving within a session; still discovering after decades.

Dholakढोलक
RhythmJoin: an afternoon

The dhol's gentler sibling — the folk heartbeat of a thousand living-room songs, ideal for seated circles. First sound: bass with one hand, edge with the other. The groove finds you.

Kanjiraकांजीरा
RhythmJoin: an afternoon

South Indian frame drum — one drum, one hand, endless depth. First sound: the shake-and-stroke groove comes fast; the masters turn it into water.

Tier Three · Masters hold these seats

Honored voices of the circle

Years of riyaz behind every note. Not handed out at the door — the masters of Mumbai hold these seats as honored guests, and the circle gathers around them. Tabla & bansuri are also the circle's bare minimum — every classical musician in India knows them, so these two seats are never empty for long.

Tablaतबला
RhythmMastery: years of riyaz

The soul of the jam — a skilled tabla player can carry a hundred strangers into one groove. In the circle: the master plays; twenty kartal and cymbals keep the same taal alongside. One rhythm, many hands.

Bansuriबाँसुरी
MelodyMastery: years

Bamboo flute — breath itself made music, floating over any jam without overpowering it. In the circle: when the master plays, the whole jam leans in.

Sitarसितार
MelodyBring your own · watch

Luminous, singing, unmistakable — and high-maintenance: carriers travel with their own, so we don't rent one unless a player asks (Aakash, Aug 4). In the circle: when a carrier walks in, the seat of honor is theirs.

The gradient is not a problem to solve. The gradient is the design.

Same taal, every hand. The tabla master and the first-time kartal player keep the same taal — one holds the architecture; twenty hold the pulse. Neither is pretending — both are true.
The drone never asks for a résumé. A stranger pumping a shruti box for the first time is doing the same job as the tanpura that has anchored concerts for centuries: holding the ground so others can fly.
Keherwa is the welcoming taal. The 8-beat folk & bhajan cycle almost every Indian jam knows. Mark the sam (beat one), and even a first-timer can feel where home is.
Every level is a real seat. Lead, harmony, rhythm, texture — the orchestra needs all four. Contribution is measured in presence, not virtuosity.

🧺 Stock the entrance with Tier One (procurement: §7) · 🎒 portable, acoustic, forgiving wins · 🪔 Indian musicians lead in Mumbai — the chart is an invitation, never a prescription · 🕉️ the score is open: corrections from carriers of these traditions are a gift.
