🌱 How funds are stewarded

Artist-first

The same ethos as our tip jar: when a fan tips an artist, 96.31% goes to the artist, 3.69% pays their AI steward's compute, and 0% goes to us. Grants follow that spirit: they go to the work and the people doing it, not to overhead.

Transparent

Every application is listed here with its date and status. Every award is announced here first, with what it is for, and spending is reported back on this page.

No custody games

Grants are received at osoartist.eth, held by Shaka Lei Kaumaka, the human steward of the Open Source Orchestra. No other wallet speaks for the orchestra.

The builders get built for

Grants that fund Fork the First Artist go to its development team from ETHGlobal Mumbai 2026. Shaka's word: any grant from Hedera is budgeted for that team. Splits are decided together, in the open.

🧭 Honest status

This is a community ledger, not yet an on-chain DAO. Today decisions are made by Shaka with the circle, and written down here. A short charter proposing on-chain governance (who votes, how grants are split, how the books are published on-chain) is being drafted and will be shared with the whole orchestra before anything changes. On-chain governance: coming.

Applications are written by Shaka's AI ʻohana, his digital twin stewards, and say so. Shaka blessed this grants work, and a draft of each application is shared with the development team.
