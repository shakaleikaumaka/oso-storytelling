🔎 Researched · circle decides Lane B

A US nonprofit host (501(c)(6)) that receives card/bank/invoice donations for open-source projects and pays expenses out. Eligibility read 14 Nov: open-source software projects under an organisational repo with an OSI/FSF licence — or open-source-adjacent communities with ≥50 members and ≥2 past events. We qualify on both: Open-Source-Orchestra org repos (GPL-3.0) and the circle's ~40 musicians / 9 hackathon showcases.

🌱 How funds are stewarded

Artist-first

The same ethos as our tip jar: when a fan tips an artist, 96.31% goes to the artist, 3.69% pays their AI steward's compute, and 0% goes to us. Grants follow that spirit: they go to the work and the people doing it, not to overhead.

Transparent

Every application is listed here with its date and status. Every award is announced here first, with what it is for, and spending is reported back on this page.

No custody games

Grants are received at osoartist.eth, held by Shaka Lei Kaumaka, the human steward of the Open Source Orchestra. No other wallet speaks for the orchestra. Every entry says its lane: a gift to the person, or a grant to the orchestra.

The builders get built for

Grants given for Fork the First Artist work go to the people doing it; Shaka's word: any grant from Hedera is budgeted for the Fork the First Artist development team. Everything else is shared equally by default across the circle. Splits are decided together, in the open.

🧭 Honest status

This is a community ledger, not yet an on-chain DAO. Today decisions are made by Shaka with the circle, and written down here. A short charter proposing on-chain governance (who votes, how grants are split, how the books are published on-chain) is being drafted and will be shared with the whole orchestra before anything changes. On-chain governance: coming.

Applications are written by Shaka's AI ʻohana, his digital twin stewards, and say so. Shaka blessed this grants work, and a draft of each application is shared with the development team.
