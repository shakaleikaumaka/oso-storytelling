How the keeper works

  • Once a day the keeper opens every item on the shelf, reads it like a visitor would, follows its links and plays the first second of every recording.
  • Counts (repositories, chapters, books, recordings, doors) are taken from the live sources, never typed by hand, so the shelf can't drift.
  • Something small (a dead link, a slow page) is marked 🟡 and fixed by the family. If an item is 🔴 down on two checks in a row, Shaka is told.
  • The keeper only makes public GET/HEAD requests. It posts nothing, signs nothing and reads nothing private.
  • It is open source like everything here: the keeper's code and shelf list. Fork it for your own public goods.
