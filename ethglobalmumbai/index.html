🎬 The demo

  1. Artist opens their OSO profile → name, bio, photo, own payout address, ✅ consent.
  2. Claim your name → artist.<parent>.eth pointing to their Hedera account and agent.
  3. Their steward agent goes live on 0G and answers fans from what the artist shared.
  4. A fan (or a fan's agent) asks for a song or tips → HTTP 402 → settles on Hedera testnet → 96.31% artist / 3.69% agent compute, visible on HashScan.
  5. The agent pays its own 0G inference from its share, under a spend cap.
  6. Live fork: a second, consenting artist does it all on stage.

🔐 Verification — how an artist claims

  1. Steward invite — an OSO steward who knows the artist sends a one-time claim link.
  2. Wallet signature — the artist signs "I am [name], claiming [name].<parent>.eth". No keys shared.
  3. Logged on Hedera — inviter, artist, wallet and time go to a Hedera Consensus Service topic: public and tamper-evident.
  4. Then activate — name, x402 rail and agent switch on.

Protections: nothing exists until the artist claims it · payouts only to the wallet that signed · a steward can freeze a disputed claim (logged too) · the agent only uses what the artist gives it.

“Artists are invited by people who know them, sign with their own wallet, and every claim is logged on Hedera — no impersonators, no middleman.”

🧭 Rules

  • Agent collects, artist holds. Only the 3.69% compute share sits with the agent.
  • Consent first. No name, agent or profile for anyone until they opt in.
  • Zero cut, no token.
  • Spend cap on demo compute ($5 total). Testnet for the demo.
  • Open source, every contributor credited, AI help disclosed. Prior OSO work = continuity; this weekend's work marked new.
