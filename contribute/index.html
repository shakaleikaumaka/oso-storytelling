Join the Orchestra:
your first open source contribution

This website has no locked door. Anyone on Earth — coder or not — can propose an improvement, and the stewards can merge it with one tap. When they do, it goes live for the whole world in about a minute.

This page teaches you how, gently, step by step. No experience needed. If you can write an email, you can do this. 🌺

Movement I · The Three Words

𝄞 Fork, Commit, Pull Request

Three funny words. Three beautiful ideas. That's the whole craft.

🍴 Fork — your own copy of the songbook

A fork is your personal copy of this website's entire source, made on your own GitHub account. Forking isn't stealing — it's how open music travels. The site even asks for it: fork me like crazy.

✍️ Commit — a saved verse

A commit is one saved change, with a little note saying what you did and why. Fix a typo? That's a commit. Translate a definition into your language? A commit. Each one is a verse in the site's history — and your name rides in that history forever.

💌 Pull Request — offering your verse to the circle

A pull request (PR) is you raising your hand: "I made something — would the orchestra like to play it?" The stewards see exactly what you changed, line by line, and can talk with you about it before anything goes live.

Movement II · Your First Contribution

𝄢 The 10-Minute Path (no code, no installs)

You can do your first contribution entirely in a web browser. Here's the whole journey:

  1. Make a free GitHub account at github.com/signup — think of it as getting your instrument.
  2. Open the orchestra's songbook: github.com/shakaleikaumaka/oso-storytelling — this repository is the website.
  3. Press the 🍴 Fork button (top right). You now have your own copy. Congratulations — you're already 80% of the way to being an open source contributor.
  4. Find the file you want to touch. The main page is index.html. Spotted a typo? Click the file, then click the ✏️ pencil icon (Edit this file).
  5. Make your change right in the browser. Small is beautiful — one fix, one improvement.
  6. Commit it. Scroll down, write a short note like fix: typo in Movement II or add: Portuguese translation of "node", and press Commit changes.
  7. Open the Pull Request. GitHub will offer a bright Contribute → Open pull request button. Press it, tell us in one or two sentences what you changed and why, and send it with aloha. 💌
  8. That's it — you're in the circle. A steward will review, maybe ask a question, and merge. ~60 seconds later, your improvement is live on opensourceorchestra.org for the whole world. 🌍
"You don't join the Open Source Orchestra. You notice you were always in it."
Movement III · The Other Side of the Circle

𝄐 How the Hub Approves — the Stewards' View

Transparency works both directions. Here's exactly what happens on our side when your PR arrives:

📬 Every PR is seen. The steward hub (the Music Space committee's site stewards and the AI ʻohana) gets notified the moment a PR opens.

🔍 We read the diff — the exact lines you changed, shown side by side. For text edits this takes minutes, not days.

💬 We talk it through — if something needs a tweak, we ask right there in the PR. Conversation, not gatekeeping.

🎻 One law protects the heart: the manifesto's philosophy changes flow through the committee, not PRs. Typos, translations, design, code, accessibility — those are wide open.

✅ Merge = live. When a steward presses merge, GitHub Pages rebuilds the site automatically. Your contribution is on the domain in about a minute, and your name is in the permanent history.

Movement IV · Ideas to Get You Started

𝄜 What Could You Offer?

🌐 Translate a definition

The 📖 Words We Play By glossary translates Web3 jargon into human. Help it speak your language — Hindi, Spanish, Portuguese, Swahili, Japanese… Mumbai welcomes all tongues.

🐛 Fix what bugs you

A typo, a broken link, a color that's hard to read, something confusing on your phone — every fix makes the door wider for the next person.

♿ Make it kinder

Accessibility improvements — alt text, contrast, keyboard navigation — are some of the most honored contributions in open source. Sanctuary means everyone gets in.

🎼 Just listen, for now

No pressure to touch code at all. Reading this page already makes you part of the circle. The instrument finds you when it's ready.
