Alaska × Subcult — live coding · Nov 4 · 1:50p

MDRN builds infrastructure for underground music: a micropayment patronage layer and a federated internet radio where any community can run a station. Subcult is where listeners pay artists directly. Someone who builds radio you can fork is going to write music you can read — on Nov 4 the typing happens in the circle.

So the circle itself can be a station: one room, one floor, everyone who shows up, broadcast as a community channel.

patio.fm — Ethereum is the radio

"A tiny live radio that broadcasts audio and video through pending Ethereum transactions. Listening needs no wallet."

Patio Station was an ETHGlobal Tokyo finalist — and says it is rebuilding an ETHDenver 2024 experiment. The tie is simple: the circle is broadcast on Ethereum itself — our circle isn’t on it yet; we’ll link it here the day it is.
