The Call They Answered

Carlota's (Next Steps) Devcon 8 Music Space letter asked five things of every artist:

  1. 🌱 A short paragraph on why you'd like to play the Music Space & join as a committee contributor.
  2. 🔗 Links to your music — tracks, sets, performances, anything that gives a feel for your sound.
  3. 🔮 A short paragraph on your vision — what your set, jam, or contribution should look & feel like.
  4. 🗓 Availability across November 3–6, so scheduling can begin.
  5. 🎻 Would you like to activate the Open Source Orchestra to support your performance?
The steward's covenant: committee contributors arrive Nov 1–2, help build & hold the space, and receive a free Devcon ticket (travel & accommodation are their own). Performing itself is voluntary and does not include a ticket — tickets are confirmed only for those who commit ahead. “A community-built stage — we're counting on stewards to help bring it to life, not just perform on it.”
