🔵 About Bluetooth, in plain words: Bluetooth audio is not a good way to get sound into a PA. It adds a delay of roughly a tenth of a second (so her voice and her track drift apart), it drops out when a room is full of phones, and it is the first thing to fail in a convention centre. The wired version of the same idea is better and costs nothing: a 3.5 mm → dual ¼" cable from her phone's headphone socket (or a USB-C adapter if her phone has no socket) straight into two line inputs. Same result, no delay, no dropouts.

We are not deciding this for her — we're asking what the Bluetooth is for, and offering the cable.
