❓ Still missing / open

  1. 🚨🚨 A KEYBOARD. This is the single most urgent ask on the whole floor. It is not on Amogh's house list. Devang brings nothing and expects one at the venue. If there is no house keyboard, he cannot play at all — and he must be told this week, not on the day, so he can borrow or hire one in Pune and travel with it.
  2. If a keyboard does appear, we need to know: how many keys (61 or 88)? weighted or not? a sustain pedal? an X-stand? a bench or stool? A professional pianist on an unweighted 49-key toy is a different (and sadder) set.
  3. 🚨 A keyboard stand — he asked for "stands", plural. X-stand + a stool.
  4. 🚨 A jack-to-jack (¼"–¼") cable — one if mono, two if stereo.
  5. Mono or stereo? (1 channel or 2.)
  6. A monitor wedge — a pianist needs to hear the instrument's own voice, and a keyboard has no acoustic sound of its own.
  7. Power for the keyboard.
  8. Does he also sing? Nothing in his words says so, but if yes, add an SM58 + stand. Ask.
