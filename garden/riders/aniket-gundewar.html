Plain words: a DI box takes an instrument's unbalanced ¼" signal and turns it into a balanced XLR one, so it can run a long cable to the board without losing level or picking up buzz. A stereo DI is simply two of those in one box, for an L+R pair.

Channels on the board: 5 — guitar L/R, iPad L/R, tabla. (The most channels any single artist needs on Nov 3.)
