The Mandala Room

Devcon 8 Music Sanctuary · Mumbai · 3–6 November 2026

Four days in one hall. On Tuesday it was an empty room: cane stools, drums on rugs, and a mandala of light on the wall. By Friday night it had held jams and singers, a lamp lit for Dhanteras, a storm of confetti, and a dance floor under the same mandala. Here are 35 frames from the official Devcon 8 photos, in the order the room lived them.

IBefore the first note

Tuesday 3 November · Day 1

The hall is empty. Cane stools stand in a circle, drums wait on Persian rugs, guitars lean on their stands, and a mandala of light already glows on the wall. Across the venue, the opening day's stage holds the first music of the week.

IIThe jams

Wednesday 4 – Friday 6 November · Days 2–4

The room fills. People rest on beanbags and the floor, players drift in and out, and the circle keeps changing shape. Singers in a saree and a kurta, saxophones in floral shirts, laughter over the hand drums.

IIIThe closing

Friday 6 November, evening · Dhanteras

On the last night the sanctuary becomes the hall for the closing ceremony. The whole room sits on the floor around the instrument circle. A brass lamp is lit for Dhanteras, the first day of Diwali. Then come the confetti, and dancing under the same mandala that watched the empty room on Tuesday.

🦋 Road to Lisbon

The next room is in Lisbon: Devcon 9, 11–13 October 2027. Portugal is where the ukulele's story begins. The little machete of Madeira sailed to Hawaiʻi on the Ravenscrag in 1879.
